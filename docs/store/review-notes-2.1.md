# App Review notes — Momentum – Men's Workout 1.0.0 (build 6)

## 1. Purpose and audience
Momentum – Men's Workout is a strength-training app for adult men who train at home or in a gym. It solves one problem: knowing what to do today. After a short set of tap-to-choose questions (goal, level, equipment, days per week, session length, focus areas) it builds a session that fits, then guides each exercise with a timer or a rep counter, a looping illustration and a form cue. It is for the general public.

## 2. Access
No account and no login. Demo account: not required.
1. Launch the app and tap Continue as Guest.
2. Tap Get Started and answer the six questions (every answer is a tap). Height and weight are optional steppers and stay on the device.
3. The "What you get" screen shows the first session. Tap Start Free.
4. Today tab: tap Start to open the workout player. Timer and Reps switch the counter. Next moves on; Finish on the last exercise saves the session on the device.
5. Progress tab: streak, workouts, minutes and this week.
6. Build tab: pick a duration and exercises for a custom session (Momentum Pro).

The app is not a hard paywall. Personalised beginner-level sessions, the workout player and Progress work with no purchase.

## 3. Paywall and purchases
Open the paywall from the Pro button at the top of Today, from See Pro Options at the end of the questions, or from Start on the Build tab. Close is at the top. Restore Purchases, Terms and Privacy are on the paywall. A subscriber can open Manage subscription from the same Pro button.

Use a sandbox Apple ID to test. Momentum Pro unlocks intermediate and advanced sessions and the custom builder.

## 4. External services
- Apple In-App Purchase.
- RevenueCat, to validate purchases and show the paywall and subscription management. It receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase history, and three training preferences (goal, level, days per week).
- No login provider, no advertising, no analytics SDK, no AI service. The app does not read or write Apple Health.

## 5. Regions
The app works the same in every region. English only.
