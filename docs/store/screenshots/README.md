# App Store screenshots

Rendered from `main` after the build 3 fixes and the redesign. Each store shot has a short caption band and an iOS status bar reading 9:41 with a full battery. No permission, settings, or restore screen is in the store set. The paywall is only in `iap-review/`, for In-App Purchase review.

The pictures are the app's own screens. Exercise art is the existing Open Exercise Illustrations (CC0) still on the Beginner session. Prices on the paywall shots are the US prices the paywall already prints when the store returns them: $2.99 a month, $19.99 a year with a 7-day trial, and $39.99 lifetime.
